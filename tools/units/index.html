Unit Conversion Tools

In science and engineering, a value without associated units is meaningless. Units provide the essential context to the numbers to understand the physical reality. The 1999 Mars Climate Orbiter disaster, caused by a mix-up between metric and imperial units, stands as a stark reminder of what happens when engineering units are inconsistent between different teams.

Reliable and automatic conversion between units is crucial in any engineering software. They eliminate the friction of manual calculations, drastically reduce human-error, ensure necessary accuracy and interoperability across global teams and diverse legacy systems.
